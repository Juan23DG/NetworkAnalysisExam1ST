## 📚 Complete Syllabus & Problem Bank Breakdown

### **Unit 1: Curves, Extrema & Asymptotes (Assignment 1, Q1–Q3)**
- **Skill 1.1**: Cubic Polynomials: Critical numbers average equals inflection point abscissa ($f(x) = ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$).
  - *Standard Variant*: $f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x + 2$ (critical numbers $1, 3$, average $2$, $x_{inf} = 2$).
  - *Challenge Variant*: $f(x) = 2x^3 + 9x^2 + 12x - 5$ (critical numbers $-1, -2$, average $-1.5$, $x_{inf} = -1.5$).
  - *General Algebraic Proof*: Vieta's root sum $x_1 + x_2 = -2b/(3a) \implies \text{avg} = -b/(3a) = x_{inf}$.
- **Skill 1.2**: Rational curve analysis:
  - $y(x) = \frac{x+1}{x^2}$: local min at $x = -2$ (value $-1/4$), inflection point at $(-3, -2/9)$, asymptotes $x = 0, y = 0$.
  - $y(x) = \frac{x-2}{x^2}$: local max at $x = 4$ (value $1/8$), inflection point at $x = 6$, asymptotes $x = 0, y = 0$.
- **Skill 1.3**: Rational curves with oblique (slant) asymptotes:
  - $y(x) = 1 - \frac{x^2}{x-1} = -x - \frac{1}{x-1}$: oblique asymptote $y = -x$, vertical asymptote $x = 1$, local min $(0, 1)$, local max $(2, -3)$, concave up on $(-\infty, 1)$, concave down on $(1, \infty)$.
  - $y(x) = 2 - \frac{x^2}{x+2}$: oblique asymptote $y = -x + 4$, vertical asymptote $x = -2$.

### **Unit 2: Constrained Optimization & Lagrange Multipliers (Assignment 1, Q4–Q6)**
- **Skill 2.1**: Physical volume & geometry maximization (formulating implicit constraints):
  - Open-top box with $S = 16\text{ cm}^2 \implies x = y = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}, z = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}, V_{max} = \frac{32\sqrt{3}}{9}\text{ cm}^3$.
  - Open-top box with $S = 27\text{ cm}^2 \implies x = y = 3\text{ cm}, z = 1.5\text{ cm}, V_{max} = 13.5\text{ cm}^3$.
  - Closed box (with lid) with $S = 54\text{ cm}^2 \implies$ cube $x = y = z = 3\text{ cm}, V_{max} = 27\text{ cm}^3$.
  - **Storage Silo Optimization**: Cylinder + hemispherical dome + flat bottom with fixed sheet metal area $S = 45\pi\text{ m}^2 \implies r=3\text{ m}, h=3\text{ m}, V_{max}=45\pi\text{ m}^3$.
  - **Trapezoidal Cooling Duct Optimization**: Sheet metal strip $L = 12\text{ cm}$ bent into 3-panel channel $\implies \theta = 60^\circ, x = 4\text{ cm}, y = 4\text{ cm}, A_{max} = 12\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^2$ (half-hexagon optimality theorem).
- **Skill 2.2**: Extreme points on intersection curves:
  - Plane $4x - 3y + 8z = 5$ and cone $z^2 = x^2 + y^2 \implies z_{min} = 5/13, z_{max} = 5/3$.
  - Plane $3x + 4y + 12z = 26$ and cone $z^2 = x^2 + y^2 \implies z_{min} = 26/17, z_{max} = 26/7$.
  - Cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and plane $x + y + z = 1 \implies z_{min} = 1 - \sqrt{2}, z_{max} = 1 + \sqrt{2}$.
- **Skill 2.3**: Closest and farthest points to geometric surfaces:
  - Sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4$ and point $(3, 1, -1) \implies \pm \frac{2}{\sqrt{11}}(3, 1, -1)$.
  - Sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$ and point $(4, -2, 4) \implies$ closest $(2, -1, 2)$ (dist $3$), farthest $(-2, 1, -2)$ (dist $9$).
  - **Shortest Engine Mounting Strut**: Point $(0,0,0)$ to 3-intercept bracket plane through $(2,0,0), (0,3,0), (0,0,6) \implies 3x+2y+z=6$; optimal connection $(9/7, 6/7, 3/7)$, minimum strut length $d = \frac{3\sqrt{14}}{7} \approx 1.6036$.

### **Unit 3: Power Series & Taylor Approximations (Assignment 1, Q7–Q10)**
- **Skill 3.1**: Radius of convergence via ratio test ($\lim |T_{n+1}/T_n| < 1$):
  - $T_n = \frac{(-5)^{2n}}{n^2 8^{n+3}}(x-2)^n \implies R = 8/25 = 0.32$.
  - $T_n = \frac{3^{2n}}{(n+2)^2 4^{n+1}}(x+3)^n \implies R = 4/9$, interval $(-31/9, -23/9)$.
- **Skill 3.2**: Taylor series expansions & Lagrange error bounds:
  - $\sin(x)$ about $\pi/4$: even cosine-like terms $+$ odd sine-like terms scaled by $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
  - $\cos(x)$ about $\pi/4$: even cosine-like terms $-$ odd sine-like terms scaled by $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
  - $\sin(x)$ about $\pi/3$: even terms scaled by $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and odd terms scaled by $\frac{1}{2}$.
  - **Logarithmic Expansion & Error Bound**: $\ln(x)$ about $a = 1 \implies \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}(x-1)^n$; $T_3(1.5) = \frac{5}{12} \approx 0.4167$ with rigorous Lagrange remainder bound $|R_3(1.5)| \le \frac{1}{64} = 0.015625$.
  - **Exponential Expansion with Chain Rule**: $f(x) = e^{2x}$ about $a = 1 \implies e^2\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{2^n}{n!}(x-1)^n$; quadratic approximation $T_2(1.1) = 1.22e^2 \approx 9.0146$ ($0.11\%$ relative error).
- **Skill 3.3**: Polynomial Taylor series remainder vanishing:
  - $f(x) = x^2 + x + 1$ about $a=2$ and $a=5$, exact equality everywhere because $f^{(n)}(x) \equiv 0$ for $n \ge 3$.
- **Skill 3.4**: Fractional power Taylor series ($f(x) = 1/\sqrt{x}$):
  - 5 terms about $a = 2$ ($1.01\%$ error) vs $a = 5$ ($14.5\%$ error) evaluated at $x = 1$, proving convergence speed scaling with normalized radius $|x-a|/R$.

### **Unit 4: Orthogonal Functions & Function Spaces (Assignment 2, Q1–Q3)**
- **Skill 4.1**: Proving orthogonality of function pairs:
  - $f_1(x) = e^x$ and $f_2(x) = \sin(x)$ on $[\pi/4, 5\pi/4]$.
  - $f_1(x) = e^{-x}$ and $f_2(x) = \cos(x)$ on $[\pi/4, 5\pi/4]$.
  - Legendre polynomials $P_1(x) = x$ and $P_3(x) = \frac{1}{2}(5x^3 - 3x)$ on $[-1, 1]$.
- **Skill 4.2**: Orthogonal sets and $L^2$ norm computation:
  - $\{\cos((2n+1)x)\}$ on $[0, \pi/2] \implies \|\phi_n\| = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} \approx 0.8862$.
- **Skill 4.3**: Generalized Pythagorean theorem in Hilbert space:
  - Proof that $\|\phi_m + \phi_n\|^2 = \|\phi_m\|^2 + \|\phi_n\|^2$ for $m \neq n$ due to vanishing inner product.
  - Linear combination of 3 orthonormal functions $\|3\phi_1 - 4\phi_2 + 12\phi_3\| = \sqrt{9 + 16 + 144} = 13$.

### **Unit 5: Real & Complex Fourier Series (Assignment 2, Q4–Q5)**
- **Skill 5.1**: Real Fourier series for piecewise & symmetric waveforms:
  - $f(x) = 0$ on $(-\pi, 0)$ and $x^2$ on $[0, \pi)$, finding $a_0 = \pi^2/3, a_n = \frac{2(-1)^n}{n^2}, b_n$.
  - $f(x) = |x|$ on $[-\pi, \pi]$ (even function), finding cosine series $f(x) = \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{4}{\pi}\sum \frac{\cos(2k-1)x}{(2k-1)^2}$.
  - **Triangular Wave Cosine Series**: $f(x) = \pi - |x|$ on $[-\pi, \pi] \implies f(x) = \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{4}{\pi}\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{\cos((2k-1)x)}{(2k-1)^2}$.
- **Skill 5.2**: Deducing numerical infinite sums via Dirichlet evaluation & Parseval's identity:
  - From $x^2$ at $x = 0 \implies \frac{\pi^2}{12} = 1 - \frac{1}{2^2} + \frac{1}{3^2} - \frac{1}{4^2} + \dots$
  - From $|x|$ at $x = 0 \implies \frac{\pi^2}{8} = 1 + \frac{1}{3^2} + \frac{1}{5^2} + \dots$ (sum of reciprocal odd squares).
  - From triangular wave $\pi - |x|$ at $x = 0 \implies \sum \frac{1}{(2k-1)^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$; partitioning odd/even terms proves the **Basel Problem** $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$.
  - **Parseval's Identity & Energy Conservation**: Actuator sawtooth wave $f(x) = x \implies$ equating time-domain mean square $\frac{\pi^2}{3}$ with spectral power $2\sum \frac{1}{n^2}$ proves $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$ without point evaluation.
  - From square wave at $x = \pi/2 \implies \frac{\pi}{4} = 1 - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{7} + \dots$ (Leibniz series).
- **Skill 5.3**: Complex Fourier series:
  - $f(x) = e^{-|x|}$ on $[-1, 1] \implies c_n = \frac{1 - (-1)^n e^{-1}}{1 + n^2\pi^2}$ with $O(1/n^2)$ quadratic frequency decay.
  - **Hyperbolic Cooling Fin**: $f(x) = \cosh(x)$ on $[-1, 1] \implies c_n = \frac{(-1)^n \sinh(1)}{1 + n^2\pi^2}$.

### **Unit 6: Vector Algebra & Geometric Applications (Assignment 2, Q6–Q8)**
- **Skill 6.1**: Initial and terminal point calculations:
  - $\vec{P_1P_2} = 4\hat{i} + 8\hat{j}, P_1(-3, 10) \implies P_2(1, 18)$.
  - $\vec{P_1P_2} = -5\hat{i} - \hat{j}, P_2(4, 7) \implies P_1(9, 8)$.
  - 3D: $\vec{P_1P_2} = 2\hat{i} - 4\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}, P_1(3, -1, 2) \implies P_2(5, -5, 7)$.
- **Skill 6.2**: Parallel vectors with specified magnitudes and closed vector polygon loops:
  - $\vec{a} = 3\hat{i} + 7\hat{j}, \|\vec{b}\| = 2 \implies \vec{b} = \pm \frac{2}{\sqrt{58}}(3\hat{i} + 7\hat{j})$.
  - $\vec{a} = 2\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + 6\hat{k}, \|\vec{b}\| = 14 \implies \vec{b} = \pm (4\hat{i} - 6\hat{j} + 12\hat{k})$.
- **Skill 6.3**: Dual-perpendicular vectors and rhombus orthogonality:
  - Rhombus diagonal proof $(\vec{a}+\vec{b})\cdot(\vec{a}-\vec{b}) = \|\vec{a}\|^2 - \|\vec{b}\|^2 = 0$.
  - Vector $\vec{V} = x_1\hat{i} + y_1\hat{j} + \hat{k}$ perpendicular to $\langle 3, 1, -1 \rangle$ and $\langle -3, 2, 2 \rangle \implies x_1 = 4/9, y_1 = -1/3$.
- **Skill 6.4**: Coplanarity test for 4 points via scalar triple product:
  - Points $P_1(1, 1, -2), P_2(4, 0, -3), P_3(1, -5, 10), P_4(-7, 2, 4) \implies \det = 0$ (coplanar).

### **Unit 7: 3D Analytic Geometry (Lines & Planes) (Assignment 2, Q9–Q10)**
- **Skill 7.1**: Line conversions and angles:
  - Convert $\frac{x}{2} = \frac{1-y}{3} = \frac{z-5}{6}$ through $(6, 4, -2) \implies x = 6+2t, y = 4-3t, z = -2+6t$.
  - Angle between $\langle -1, 2, -2 \rangle$ and $\langle 2, 3, -6 \rangle \implies \cos\theta = 16/21 \approx 40.37^\circ$.
  - Angle between $\langle 2, -1, 2 \rangle$ and $\langle 1, 2, 2 \rangle \implies \cos\theta = 4/9 \approx 63.61^\circ$.
- **Skill 7.2**: Plane containing two intersecting lines:
  - Lines through $(1, 1, 2)$ with $\vec{v}_1 = \langle 3, -1, 1 \rangle, \vec{v}_2 = \langle 4, 2, 1 \rangle \implies 3x - y - 10z = -18$.
  - Lines through $(2, 1, 3)$ with $\vec{v}_1 = \langle 1, -2, 3 \rangle, \vec{v}_2 = \langle 2, 1, -1 \rangle \implies x - 7y - 5z = -20$.
- **Skill 7.3**: Plane containing a line and perpendicular to another plane:
  - Line $\langle 3, -1, 5 \rangle$ and plane $x + y + z = 7 \implies 3x - y - 2z = 10$.
  - Line $\langle 2, -1, 3 \rangle$ and plane $2x - 3y + z = 5 \implies 2x + y - z = 1$.
